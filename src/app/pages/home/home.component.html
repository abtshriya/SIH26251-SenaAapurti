TACTICAL INTELLIGENCE

Artificial Intelligence in Military Logistics

Transforming raw defense telemetry into anticipatory tactical operational superiority

📊

1. Predictive Analytics

Continuous ingestion of multi-dimensional signals—past 15-year consumption ledgers, troop numbers, training exercise calendars, altitude profiles, and equipment wear factors—to model dynamic burn rates across varying theater defense states.

📈

2. Demand Forecasting

Deep Recurrent Neural Networks (LSTM) model non-linear seasonal consumption surges. When temperatures drop below -25°C, heating kerosene and sub-zero fuel burn rises exponentially; the model projects exact requirements 60 to 90 days ahead.

⚠️

3. Risk Prediction & Vulnerability Scoring

Automated Bayesian risk engines calculate stockout probabilities for every forward picket, factoring in road pass closure probabilities, convoy transit times, and current reserve buffers.

🗺️

4. Route Intelligence

Real-time pathfinding algorithms integrated with satellite Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) imagery and Border Roads Organisation (BRO) ground sensors evaluate road stability, slope gradient, and ice depth to avoid danger sectors.

⏱️

5. ETA Prediction Engine

Machine learning models calculate precise convoy arrival times taking into account high-altitude engine de-rating, convoy vehicle density, snow chain mounting delays, and military police checkpoint clearance speeds.

💡

6. Explainable Recommendations (XAI)

No "black-box" decision-making. Every recommendation generated by SenaAapurti presents commanders with contributing factors, confidence percentages, expected impacts, and vetted alternatives.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How does SenaAapurti maintain functionality during complete satellite blackout?

A: The platform operates with local encrypted SQLite databases on field terminals. As soon as line-of-sight VHF/UHF or ASCON connectivity is re-established, vouchers auto-reconcile using cryptographic delta merges.

Q: What is the procedure for emergency ammunition replenishment during exercises?

A: Officers with Level 2 clearance can file a Flash SOS requisition directly from the Unit Portal, which automatically alerts DGOS and assigns immediate tasking to nearest depots.
