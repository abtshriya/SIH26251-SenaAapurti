About SenaAapurti

1. Introduction

SenaAapurti (सेना आपूर्ति) is the national predictive logistics backbone engineered for the Indian Army by the Directorate General of Ordnance Services (DGOS) under the Master General of Sustenance (MGS) Branch, Integrated HQ of MoD (Army). The platform unites tactical military formations, ordnance factories, Central Ordnance Depots (CODs), and forward supply nodes into a synchronized, autonomous digital continuum.

2. Problem Statement

The Indian Army defends extreme, heterogeneous geographies ranging from high-altitude arctic glaciers at Siachen and eastern Ladakh (-40°C, above 18,000 ft) to dense Himalayan jungle valleys in Arunachal Pradesh and the Thar desert. Seasonal passes freeze shut for 5 to 7 months annually. Traditional logistics suffered from:

  • Reactive Replenishment: Supplies were requisitioned only after field stocks reached critically low levels.
  • Single-Point Choke Points: Landslides and avalanches on vital passes (Zojila, Khardung La, Sela Pass) repeatedly blocked surface columns.
  • Information Silos: Lack of real-time unified visibility across Central Depots and forward foxhole stores.

3. Solution Architecture

SenaAapurti introduces continuous sensor-driven data fusion, automated burn-rate telematics, satellite meteorology feeds, and machine learning neural networks to predict consumption 30 to 90 days in advance. It autonomously routes supplies along safe bypass corridors and triggers pre-winter reserve stocking before high-altitude passes freeze shut.

4. Strategic Objectives

Zero Stockout Guarantee

Eliminate emergency ammunition and fuel shortfalls during high-tempo combat exercises or cold waves.

Total Asset Visibility

Real-time digital audit of Class I to Class IX military inventory across all 7 Operational Commands.

Optimized Transit Timelines

Reduce road column delays by 42% through AI-calculated bypass axes and BRO sensor telemetry.

5. Core Principles

  • Anticipatory Provisioning: Supply arrives before the requirement becomes critical.
  • Cryptographic Security: All data packets are encrypted under military-grade standards (ASCON/SATCOM).
  • Explainable Intelligence: AI recommendations provide clear tactical rationales for commanders.
  • All-Weather Resilience: Fallback offline synchronization for isolated forward pickets.

AI in Military Logistics

How artificial intelligence transforms raw defense telemetry into proactive tactical superiority:

1. Predictive Analytics

Continuous ingestion of multi-dimensional signals—past 15-year consumption ledgers, troop numbers, training exercise calendars, altitude profiles, and equipment wear factors—to model dynamic burn rates across varying theater defense states.

2. Demand Forecasting

Deep Recurrent Neural Networks (LSTM) model non-linear seasonal consumption surges. For example, when temperatures drop below -25°C, heating kerosene and sub-zero fuel burn rises exponentially; the model projects exact gallon requirements 60 days ahead.

3. Risk Prediction & Vulnerability Scoring

Automated Bayesian risk engines calculate stockout probabilities for every forward picket, factoring in road pass closure probabilities, convoy transit times, and current reserve buffers.

4. Route Intelligence

Real-time pathfinding algorithms integrated with satellite Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) imagery and Border Roads Organisation (BRO) ground sensors evaluate road stability, slope gradient, and ice depth to avoid danger sectors.

5. ETA Prediction Engine

Machine learning models calculate precise convoy arrival times taking into account high-altitude engine de-rating, convoy vehicle density, snow chain mounting delays, and military police checkpoint clearance speeds.

6. Explainable Recommendations (XAI)

No "black-box" decision-making. Every recommendation generated by SenaAapurti presents commanders with contributing factors, confidence percentages, expected impacts, and vetted alternatives.

User Guidelines & Operating Manuals

Comprehensive standard operating procedures for military logisticians and commanders:

1. Command User Guide

Designed for Army HQ, Theater Commands, and Corps Ordnance Officers. Covers accessing the National Readiness HUD, reviewing theater-wide Days of Supply (DOS), executing inter-depot redistributions, and authorizing AI alert remedies.

2. Unit User Guide

Designed for Regimental Quartermasters (RQM) and Battalion Logistics Officers. Explains recording daily store receipts, auditing ammunition basic loads (UBL), managing winter clothing scales, and dispatching SOS emergency requests.

3. Login & Authentication Guide

Step-by-step instructions for military two-factor authentication (2FA), entering Army Service Numbers (e.g. IC-XXXXXX), verifying security CAPTCHA, and handling forgotten passwords or token resets.

4. Inventory Management Guide

SOP for classifying store ledgers across Class I (Rations), Class III (POL), Class V (Munitions), and Class IX (Armor Spares). Explains setting safety buffers and reorder thresholds.

5. Supply Request Guide

How to draft, submit, and track formal requisition indents. Details urgency classifications: Routine, Operational Priority 1, and Flash SOS Emergency.

6. Logistics Movement Guide

Guidelines for Convoy Escort Commanders (ASC/AOC), monitoring convoy telematics, reporting road blockage checkpoints, and mounting snow chains.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How does SenaAapurti maintain functionality during complete satellite blackout?

A: The platform operates with local encrypted SQLite databases on field terminals. As soon as line-of-sight VHF/UHF or ASCON connectivity is re-established, vouchers auto-reconcile using cryptographic delta merges.

Q: What is the procedure for emergency ammunition replenishment during exercises?

A: Officers with Level 2 clearance can file a Flash SOS requisition directly from the Unit Portal, which automatically alerts DGOS and assigns immediate tasking to nearest depots.
