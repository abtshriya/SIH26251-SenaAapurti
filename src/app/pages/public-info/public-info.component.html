Genesis & Mandate of Project SenaAapurti

SenaAapurti is the flagship national defense logistics initiative spearheaded by the Directorate General of Ordnance Services (DGOS) under the aegis of the Master General of Sustenance (MGS) Branch, Integrated Headquarters of Ministry of Defence (Army).

"Logistics is the lifeblood of warfare. In high-altitude battlegrounds like the Siachen Glacier, eastern Ladakh, and the dense jungles of the North-East, victory belongs to the force that can sustain its combatants without interruption."

— Integrated Logistics Doctrine, Indian Army

Strategic Pillars

From Reactive to Predictive

Moving away from manual post-consumption replenishment to algorithmically driven pre-positioning of munitions, cold weather fuels, and rations.

Depot-to-Foxhole Visibility

End-to-end telemetry connecting Central Ordnance Depots (CODs), Advance Base Depots, and Field Supply Depots down to isolated forward defiles.

All-Weather Route Resilience

Sensor integration across high-altitude mountain passes (Zojila, Khardung La, Chang La, Sela Tunnel) for dynamic hazard avoidance.

Tri-Services Synchronization

SenaAapurti provides native APIs for inter-service coordination with the Indian Air Force (Air Logistics Wing for C-17, C-130J, AN-32, and Mi-17 heavy aerial drops) and Indian Navy (Naval Armament Depots and coastal supply lines), creating an unshakeable theater command logistics network.

Next-Generation AI & Autonomous Decision Systems

SenaAapurti embeds cutting-edge machine learning and predictive modeling directly into the defense supply chain, transforming logistics planning into an impenetrable force multiplier.

📊

Winter Burn Rate Forecasting

Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs) analyze 15 years of meteorological data, troop deployment densities, and historical cold-weather consumption to forecast fuel, kerosene, and ration burn with 96.8% accuracy.

🛰️

Satellite Terrain & Avalanche Prediction

Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) imagery coupled with Border Roads Organisation (BRO) ground sensors identifies slope instability and road subsidence hours before landslides occur, rerouting convoys automatically.

📦

Computer Vision Ordnance Auditing

Edge-AI cameras installed at Central Ordnance Depots rapidly tally ammunition crates, inspect tamper seals, and detect micro-corrosion on mortar and artillery projectiles in seconds.

🚁

Autonomous Drone Swarm Logistics

Integrated routing algorithms for heavy-lift defense logistics drones capable of carrying 50kg to 150kg payloads to forward Siachen pickets in sub-zero blizzard conditions.

What-If Wargame Scenario Engine

Logistics commanders can simulate full-scale multi-front mobilization scenarios, severe railhead disruptions, or fuel depot strikes. The AI engine recalculates alternative pass throughputs and automatically drafts airlift sortie taskings.

Comprehensive Platform Capabilities

An integrated suite of 8 core modular capabilities designed to support Army Formations across tactical, operational, and strategic levels:

01

Predictive Demand Forecasting

Predicts localized demand spikes up to 90 days in advance based on seasonal weather, altitude gradients, and exercise schedules.

02

Inventory Intelligence & Smart Barcoding

Automated RFID tagging and QR-coded Issue Vouchers (IV) eliminate ghost inventories and ensure zero-delay store reconciliation.

03

Logistics Movement Tracking & Telemetry

Continuous transponder monitoring of ALS, Tatra 8x8, and civilian hired transport vehicles with speed, cargo temperature, and escort telemetry.

04

Route Optimization & Risk Analysis

Real-time bypass generation around blocked mountain passes (e.g. Atal Tunnel bypass, Shinku La axis, Rohtang corridors).

05

Weather & Terrain Intelligence

Sensor feeds from DRDO snow & avalanche study establishments (SASE), IMD mountain radars, and forward weather stations.

06

AI-Driven Replenishment Recommendations

Proactive alerts to Quartermasters recommending early requisitioning before passes close for the 6-month winter stocking window.

07

What-If Crisis Simulation

Simulates worst-case logistical bottlenecks, bridge collapses, and theater ammunition surges to test replenishment viability.

08

Executive Defense Analytics & Audit

Real-time dashboards for the Chief of Army Staff (COAS), Vice Chief (VCOAS), and Army Commanders with national readiness scores.

Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

Guidelines for Unit Quartermasters (RQM), Depot Commandants, and Convoy Escort Commanders:

SOP 14/A: Indent Requisition Lifecycles

All indents must be categorized into Routine (Class I to IX), Operational Priority 1 (Forward border deployment), or SOS Emergency. Digital vouchers must be countersigned using defense smartcards.

SOP 28/W: High-Altitude Winter Stocking Protocol

Winter stocking for Ladakh (14 Corps) and Siachen sectors must achieve 100% pre-positioning by 15 October annually. Sub-zero grade High-Speed Diesel (-40°C pour point) and arctic multi-layer clothing must be verified via chemical analysis.

SOP 39/C: Convoy Safety & Breakdown Procedures

Convoys exceeding 10 vehicles must maintain a recovery ALS vehicle and medical escort. In the event of icing on mountain passes, snow chains must be deployed immediately.
